## 2. eMudhra intermediate CA compromise
- **Area:** Cybersecurity
- **Summary:** Indian CA eMudhra disclosed that its intermediate certificate keys were compromised (one private key was literally the number "4"); browsers (Mozilla Bugzilla filed) are moving to remove its intermediates from the trust store. Scott Helme's follow-up details the key being cracked in milliseconds.
- **Why it matters:** Any service, API, or mTLS deployment that pins or trusts eMudhra-issued certs needs review now. A good forcing function to audit your PKI trust chain, cert pinning, and CAA records.
- **HN:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50166579
- **Source:** https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/
